# v0.3.0 · Repeatable exchanges and global frontier

User feedback (2026-11-10): identify exchange points with a loop symbol, and replace the short portal ending with ongoing planet expansion.

- All three original exchange points and all frontier exchanges show a rotating ↻ badge, explicit input → output model icons, and a “repeats while standing” context prompt. Reduced-motion preferences stop the badge animation.
- Original campaign, terrain and progression remain available. The portal unlocks EXPAND PLANET, also available to completed v1/v2 saves without replaying. The original island is the home base, and the frontier is a new full spherical stage.
- Each frontier planet has 642 closed geodesic cells (630 hexagons, 12 pentagons). It starts with 19 connected cells, all five renewable raw inputs and three exchange stations. The player can walk around the back and either pole without a longitude seam.
- Tap a border cost to select it; the delivery pad stays on reachable owned ground. Only actual inventory is spent. Partial deliveries persist. Nearest goal finds raw materials or the exchange station. Auto-harvest, drag, tap navigation and solid resource collision remain.
- Grassland, quarry, sand, ruins and snow biomes vary by planet seed. Coverage milestones at 25%, 50%, 75% raise yield from 3 to 4/5/6 and speed exchanges; recipe prices stay the same. At 100%, NEXT PLANET creates a new layout. Backpack and iron tools persist; coverage bonuses are per planet.
- Home island and current planet save together. Completed planet count is represented by the current planet number; older completed planets are not stored as revisitable worlds. Generation and object storage stay bounded. No claim of literally infinite stored geometry.
- Frontier terrain is batched into one mesh; resource props outside the local area sleep, and the overview hides those props. Camera heading parallel-transports over the sphere. Original models, font, palette and sound are reused.

Local source only; no GitHub or Loopit publication is authorized or performed for this revision.

Verification: see QA-v03.md. QA simulated duration is not human playtime.
