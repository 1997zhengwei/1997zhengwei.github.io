# Sponge Shift Express — adventure revision

User problem: each original six-gate district ended in seconds and three shape states alone were repetitive. Preserve easy controls and recognizable 3D characters, but make choices affect outcomes.

Core loop: read upcoming lanes → choose a safe route or match a rewarding obstacle → charge a shape ability → dodge or defeat an enemy → deliver to a familiar character → choose an upgrade → continue from the saved stop. Three 514-unit districts, 24 rows each, nine deliveries total. Approximately five minutes of driving per whole run.

Progression is mechanical: magnet collects across lanes, efficient abilities lower energy costs, shield blocks damage. Shape powers change speed/protection, solid collisions or jumping. Safe routes give less score; challenging routes refill abilities. Delivery stops prevent long retries and offer a clear destination. Express replay remixes route options.

Keep three form buttons, lane selection and one context-sensitive ability button. No crafting menu, inventory management or complex skill tree. First gates teach by waiting, enemies get explicit DODGE/ENEMY labels, delivery lanes wait safely. English player text; 390×780 and 400×580 local browser checks.

Apply loopit-game-creator's contextual guidance, visible progression and learnable failure principles. Local development and actual playthrough first; external upload and platform music/Power polish require the later authorized stage.
